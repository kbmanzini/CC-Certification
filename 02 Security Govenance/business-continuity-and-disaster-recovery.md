# Business Continuity (BC)

## What Is Business Continuity?

Business continuity is an organization's ability to continue delivering essential products or services during and after a disruption.

The focus is on keeping critical business functions operating.

## Examples of Disruptions

* Power outages
* Network failures
* Severe weather
* Building damage
* Supplier interruptions
* Cybersecurity incidents
* Loss of access to important systems

## Business Continuity Plan (BCP)

A Business Continuity Plan describes how an organization will maintain or restore essential business operations during a disruption.

A BCP may identify:

* Critical business functions
* Important personnel and responsibilities
* Alternative work locations
* Communication procedures
* Backup equipment and resources
* Dependencies on technology and suppliers
* Procedures for continuing essential services

## Example

A company loses access to its primary office because of a power outage.

Employees use an approved alternative work location and backup communication systems to continue essential operations.

This is business continuity because the organization is maintaining its important functions.

## Business Continuity vs. Disaster Recovery

### Business Continuity

Focuses on keeping essential business operations running.

### Disaster Recovery

Focuses on restoring IT systems, applications, infrastructure, and data after a disruption.

Disaster recovery often supports the larger business continuity effort.

## CC Exam Tips

If a question asks how an organization continues essential services during a disruption, think:

**Business Continuity**

## What I Learned

Business continuity helps an organization maintain critical services even when normal operations are disrupted.

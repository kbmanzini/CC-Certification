# Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

## What Is a Business Impact Analysis?

A Business Impact Analysis is a process used to identify critical business functions and understand how disruptions could affect an organization.

It helps determine which activities need to be restored first and how quickly they need to recover.

## What a BIA Examines

A BIA may consider:

* Financial losses
* Operational interruptions
* Customer impact
* Legal or regulatory consequences
* Reputational damage
* Dependencies between systems and departments
* Resources needed to restore operations

## Critical Business Functions

Critical business functions are activities that are especially important to an organization's operations.

### Example

For an online store, critical functions might include:

* Processing customer orders
* Accepting payments
* Maintaining inventory records
* Providing customer support

A temporary interruption to these services could affect revenue and customers.

## Prioritization

A BIA helps an organization decide which systems, services, and processes should receive recovery priority.

Not every system has the same importance or recovery requirements.

## BIA vs. Risk Assessment

### Business Impact Analysis

Focuses on the consequences of business functions being interrupted.

### Risk Assessment

Focuses on identifying and evaluating risks, including their likelihood and potential impact.

These processes can support each other but are not identical.

## CC Exam Tips

If a question asks which process identifies critical business functions and evaluates the effects of interruptions, think:

**Business Impact Analysis**

## What I Learned

A BIA helps an organization understand the consequences of disruption and prioritize recovery efforts.

# Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)

## What Is GRC?

GRC stands for:

* **Governance**
* **Risk Management**
* **Compliance**

GRC helps an organization manage security, make responsible decisions, understand risks, and meet applicable requirements.

## Governance

Governance is how an organization directs and oversees its activities.

In cybersecurity, governance establishes security responsibilities, expectations, and decision-making processes.

### Examples

* Establishing security policies
* Assigning security responsibilities
* Defining who can approve security decisions
* Having leadership review security performance

## Risk Management

Risk management is the process of identifying, evaluating, treating, and monitoring risks.

### Example

A company discovers that its employees use outdated software.

The organization evaluates the risk and decides to update the software to reduce the chance of a security incident.

### Common Risk Treatments

* **Avoid:** Stop the activity that creates the risk.
* **Mitigate:** Reduce the likelihood or impact of the risk.
* **Transfer:** Shift some financial risk to another party, such as through insurance.
* **Accept:** Acknowledge the risk and decide to live with it.

## Compliance

Compliance means following applicable laws, regulations, contractual obligations, and organizational requirements.

### Examples

* Following applicable privacy laws
* Meeting contractual security requirements
* Following internal security policies
* Meeting applicable industry requirements

## Frameworks and Tools

A framework provides organized guidance for managing cybersecurity risks and security activities.

Examples include:

* NIST Cybersecurity Framework
* ISO/IEC 27001
* Center for Internet Security (CIS) Critical Security Controls

Organizations select frameworks and tools based on their needs, risks, and applicable requirements.

## Why GRC Matters

GRC helps organizations:

* Establish clear security responsibilities
* Make informed decisions about risk
* Follow applicable requirements
* Track security improvements
* Coordinate security activities across departments

## CC Exam Tips

Remember the difference:

* **Governance:** How security is directed and overseen.
* **Risk management:** How risks are identified and handled.
* **Compliance:** Whether applicable requirements are being met.

## What I Learned

GRC brings governance, risk management, and compliance together so an organization can manage cybersecurity in an organized and accountable way.

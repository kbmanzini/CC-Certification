# Incident Response

## What Is Incident Response?

Incident response is the organized process an organization uses to prepare for, identify, manage, and recover from cybersecurity incidents.

The goal is to limit harm, restore operations, and learn from what happened.

## Why Incident Response Matters

A coordinated response can help an organization:

* Reduce damage
* Limit the spread of an incident
* Protect sensitive information
* Restore affected services
* Communicate with the right people
* Preserve relevant evidence
* Prevent similar incidents in the future

## Incident Response Plan

An Incident Response Plan documents how an organization handles cybersecurity incidents.

It may identify:

* Roles and responsibilities
* Reporting procedures
* Escalation requirements
* Communication channels
* Investigation and containment procedures
* Recovery procedures
* Documentation requirements
* Contact information

## Common Incident Types

* Malware infections
* Unauthorized account access
* Data exposure
* Phishing-related compromise
* Service disruptions
* Unauthorized system changes

## Roles and Communication

Incident response may involve IT staff, security personnel, management, legal teams, communications staff, and external specialists.

Each organization should define who has authority to make decisions and who needs to be informed.

## Documentation

Incident responders should maintain accurate records of important observations, decisions, actions, and timelines.

Documentation supports investigation, accountability, reporting, and future improvements.

## Example

A security monitoring system alerts an organization to suspicious activity. The security team investigates, follows its incident-response procedures, and coordinates the appropriate containment and recovery actions.

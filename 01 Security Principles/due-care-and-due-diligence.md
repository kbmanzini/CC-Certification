# Due Care and Due Diligence

## Due Care

Due care means taking **reasonable actions to protect people, systems, and information** from known or foreseeable risks.

In simple terms:

> **Due care = Doing the right things to protect security.**

### Examples

- Installing security updates
- Using appropriate security controls
- Creating security policies
- Training employees
- Protecting sensitive information
- Responding to known security risks
- Following applicable laws and regulations

### Example

A company knows that an important server has a critical security vulnerability.

Taking reasonable action to patch the server is an example of **due care**.

---

## Due Diligence

Due diligence means **investigating, evaluating, and continuously monitoring security risks and controls**.

In simple terms:

> **Due diligence = Checking that security is actually being handled properly.**

### Examples

- Researching security risks
- Evaluating vendors before using their services
- Reviewing security controls
- Monitoring systems
- Performing security assessments
- Investigating incidents
- Regularly reviewing policies

### Example

A company regularly reviews its security controls to determine whether they are still effective.

This is an example of **due diligence**.

---

## Due Care vs. Due Diligence

| Concept | Meaning | Easy Memory |
|---|---|---|
| Due Care | Taking reasonable protective actions | **Do the right things** |
| Due Diligence | Investigating, checking, and monitoring | **Keep checking** |

### Easy Example

Imagine a company wants to protect its computers.

**Due care:**
The company installs security updates.

**Due diligence:**
The company regularly checks whether systems are patched and whether new vulnerabilities have appeared.

Both are important.

---

## Why They Matter

Organizations have a responsibility to take reasonable steps to protect:

- Information
- Systems
- Employees
- Customers
- Business operations

Failing to take reasonable security precautions can increase the organization's risk.

---

## CC Exam Tip

If a question focuses on:

**Taking reasonable security precautions**

Think:

**Due Care**

If a question focuses on:

**Investigating, reviewing, evaluating, or monitoring security**

Think:

**Due Diligence**

### Remember

**Due Care = Do**

**Due Diligence = Check**

---

## What I Learned

Due care is about taking reasonable actions to protect an organization.

Due diligence is about investigating, evaluating, and monitoring security to make sure those protections continue to address risks.

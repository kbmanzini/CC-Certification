# Professional Conduct

## What It Means

Professional conduct refers to behaving responsibly, ethically, legally, and professionally when performing cybersecurity work.

In simple terms:

> **Professional conduct = Do cybersecurity work responsibly**

---

## Important Principles

Cybersecurity professionals should:

- Follow applicable laws
- Follow organizational policies
- Work within their authorization
- Protect confidential information
- Be honest about their skills
- Act responsibly
- Respect other people
- Continue developing their knowledge
- Avoid unnecessary harm

---

## Authorization

A cybersecurity professional should only perform activities they are authorized to perform.

### Example

A security tester is hired to test a company's website.

The tester should stay within the scope of the approved test.

Finding another unrelated system does not automatically give the tester permission to test it.

> **Skill does not equal permission.**

---

## Protecting Confidential Information

Cybersecurity professionals may have access to sensitive information.

They should protect that information and only use it for legitimate authorized purposes.

---

## Honesty

Professionals should accurately represent:

- Skills
- Experience
- Qualifications
- Results
- Work performed

They should not claim to have expertise or certifications they do not have.

---

## Continuing Education

Cybersecurity changes quickly.

Professionals should continue learning about:

- New technologies
- New vulnerabilities
- New threats
- Security practices
- Laws and requirements

---

## CC Exam Tip

Professional conduct means performing cybersecurity work:

**Legally + Ethically + Responsibly + Within Authorization**

---

## What I Learned

# Governance

## What It Means

Security governance is the system of **rules, responsibilities, decisions, and oversight** used to direct an organization's security program.

In simple terms:

> **Governance = How an organization directs and manages security**

---

## What Governance Helps Establish

Governance can establish:

- Security responsibilities
- Organizational policies
- Security objectives
- Risk management expectations
- Compliance requirements
- Accountability
- Oversight

---

## Common Governance Documents

### Policy

A policy describes **what the organization requires or expects**.

**Example:**

Employees must protect company information.

### Standard

A standard provides a **specific requirement** that supports a policy.

**Example:**

Passwords must meet specific length and complexity requirements.

### Procedure

A procedure explains **how to perform a task**.

**Example:**

Steps an administrator follows to create a new employee account.

---

## Governance vs. Management

Governance focuses on:

- Direction
- Oversight
- Responsibilities
- Requirements
- Organizational objectives

Management focuses more on carrying out those requirements and operating the security program.

---

## Example

An organization decides that protecting customer information is a major security objective.

Leadership establishes policies and assigns responsibilities.

Security teams then implement controls and procedures to meet those requirements.

This demonstrates governance and management working together.

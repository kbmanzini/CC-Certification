# Privacy

## What It Means

Privacy involves the proper handling and protection of information about individuals.

This is especially important when dealing with **personally identifiable information (PII)**.

In simple terms:

> **Privacy = Proper handling of information about people**

---

## Personally Identifiable Information

PII is information that can identify or relate to an individual.

Examples can include:

- Name
- Address
- Phone number
- Email address
- Government identification number
- Financial information
- Other information associated with an identifiable person

---

## Privacy vs. Confidentiality

These concepts are related but not identical.

### Privacy

Focuses on how information about individuals is:

- Collected
- Used
- Stored
- Shared
- Protected
- Disposed of

### Confidentiality

Focuses on preventing unauthorized people from accessing information.

### Example

A company may have a rule that customer information should only be collected for a specific purpose.

That is a privacy concern.

A system preventing unauthorized employees from viewing the customer information is a confidentiality control.

---

## Why Privacy Matters

Organizations may have legal, regulatory, contractual, and ethical responsibilities regarding personal information.

Poor privacy practices can expose individuals to risks such as:

- Identity theft
- Fraud
- Unwanted disclosure
- Loss of control over personal information

---

## CC Exam Tip

If a question focuses on the proper handling of information about individuals, think:

**Privacy**

If it focuses on preventing unauthorized access to information, think:

**Confidentiality**

# Risk Management

## What It Means

Risk management is the process of identifying, assessing, treating, and monitoring risks.

In simple terms:

> **Risk management = Understanding and managing potential problems**

---

# Risk

Risk is the possibility that a threat will exploit a vulnerability and cause harm.

A simple way to think about it is:

**Threat + Vulnerability + Potential Impact = Risk**

---

## Threat

A threat is something that could cause harm.

Examples:

- Malware
- Attackers
- Natural disasters
- Insider threats
- Hardware failure

---

## Vulnerability

A vulnerability is a weakness that could be exploited.

Examples:

- Unpatched software
- Weak passwords
- Misconfigured systems
- Excessive permissions

---

## Impact

Impact describes the consequences if something goes wrong.

Examples:

- Financial loss
- Data loss
- Service interruption
- Privacy violation
- Damage to reputation

---

# Risk Management Process

A basic risk management process includes:

1. Identify risks
2. Assess risks
3. Treat risks
4. Monitor risks

---

## 1. Identify

Determine what risks exist.

### Example

A company discovers that an important server is running outdated software.

---

## 2. Assess

Determine how serious the risk is.

Organizations may consider:

- Likelihood
- Potential impact
- Importance of the affected asset

---

## 3. Treat

Choose how to handle the risk.

Common approaches include:

### Avoid

Stop the activity that creates the risk.

### Mitigate

Reduce the likelihood or impact of the risk.

### Transfer

Shift some or all of the financial or operational consequences to another party.

### Accept

Recognize the risk and decide that no additional action is currently necessary.

---

## 4. Monitor

Continue watching the risk because circumstances can change.

A previously acceptable risk may become more serious if:

- New threats appear
- Vulnerabilities are discovered
- Systems change
- Business requirements change

---

# Risk Tolerance

Risk tolerance is the amount or level of risk an organization is willing to accept.

Different organizations may have different levels of risk tolerance.

---

## Example

A company discovers a vulnerability in an internal system.

It could:

- Fix the vulnerability
- Add another security control
- Stop using the vulnerable system
- Transfer some risk through a third party
- Accept the remaining risk

The appropriate choice depends on the organization's circumstances and risk requirements.

---

## CC Exam Tip

Remember:

**Identify → Assess → Treat → Monitor**

And remember the common treatment options:

**Avoid → Mitigate → Transfer → Accept**

---

## What I Learned

Risk management helps organizations understand potential threats and vulnerabilities, evaluate their possible impact, choose how to handle risks, and continue monitoring them.

# Security Principles Review

This file provides a quick review of the major Security Principles concepts.

---

## CIA Triad

### Confidentiality

Prevents unauthorized disclosure of information.

**Remember:** Who can see it?

### Integrity

Protects information from unauthorized or improper changes.

**Remember:** Can I trust that it has not been changed?

### Availability

Ensures authorized users can access information and systems when needed.

**Remember:** Can I access it?

---

# Authentication, Authorization, and Accounting

### Authentication

Proves identity.

**Who are you?**

### Authorization

Determines permissions.

**What can you do?**

### Accounting

Records activity.

**What did you do?**

---

# Non-Repudiation

Provides evidence connecting an action or transaction to its source.

**Remember:** Evidence of who performed an action.

---

# Privacy

Focuses on the proper handling of information about individuals.

**Remember:** Protect people's information appropriately.

---

# Adequate Security

Security protections should be appropriate for the level of risk and potential harm.

**Remember:** Security should match the risk.

---

# Risk Management

Basic process:

**Identify → Assess → Treat → Monitor**

Common risk treatments:

**Avoid → Mitigate → Transfer → Accept**

---

# Governance

Provides organizational direction, rules, responsibilities, and oversight for security.

**Remember:** Governance directs security.

---

# Security Controls

Three broad categories:

### Administrative

Rules, policies, and procedures.

### Technical

Technology-based protection.

### Physical

Physical protection.

---

# Due Care

Taking reasonable actions to protect systems and information.

**Remember:** Do the right things.

---

# Due Diligence

Investigating, evaluating, and monitoring security.

**Remember:** Keep checking.

---

# Professional Conduct

Cybersecurity professionals should:

- Follow laws
- Follow policies
- Work within authorization
- Protect information
- Be honest
- Act responsibly
- Continue learning

**Remember:** Skill does not equal permission.

---

# ISC2 Code of Ethics

Cybersecurity professionals should act:

- Ethically
- Honestly
- Responsibly
- Legally
- Competently

They should protect people, systems, organizations, and the public.

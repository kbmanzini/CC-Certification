# ISC2 Code of Ethics

## Purpose

The ISC2 Code of Ethics provides ethical principles for cybersecurity professionals.

It helps professionals understand their responsibilities to:

- Society
- The public
- Organizations
- Clients
- Colleagues
- The cybersecurity profession

---

## Important Ethical Principles

Cybersecurity professionals should:

- Act ethically
- Protect society and the public good
- Protect infrastructure
- Act honestly and responsibly
- Follow applicable laws
- Provide competent and diligent service
- Protect the profession
- Maintain professional behavior

---

## Protecting the Public

Cybersecurity professionals can have significant access to systems and information.

Their actions can affect:

- Individuals
- Organizations
- Businesses
- Critical infrastructure
- The public

Professionals should therefore consider the potential consequences of their actions.

---

## Honesty and Responsibility

Cybersecurity professionals should be honest about:

- Their skills
- Their experience
- Their responsibilities
- Their actions

They should not misrepresent their abilities or intentionally deceive others.

---

## Competence

Cybersecurity professionals should provide services within their level of knowledge and ability.

They should continue learning as technology and threats change.

---

## Legal and Ethical Behavior

Being technically capable of doing something does not automatically mean you have permission to do it.

### Example

A security professional discovers a vulnerability in a company's server.

If they are not authorized to test or access that system, they should not exploit the vulnerability.

> **Skill does not equal permission.**

# Physical Controls

## What They Are

Physical controls are security controls that protect people, buildings, equipment, and physical areas.

In simple terms:

> **Physical controls = Physical protection**

---

## Examples

Common physical controls include:

- Locks
- Doors
- Fences
- Security guards
- Security cameras
- Badge readers
- Mantraps
- Lighting
- Bollards
- Gates
- Barriers

---

## Example

A company keeps its servers inside a locked room.

Only authorized employees have access to the room.

The locked room is a physical security control.

---

## Physical Access

Physical security can help prevent unauthorized people from:

- Entering restricted areas
- Accessing equipment
- Stealing hardware
- Damaging systems
- Tampering with devices

---

## Mantrap

A mantrap is a controlled entry area designed to prevent unauthorized people from following an authorized person through a secure entrance.

It commonly uses two doors that are controlled so that both doors are not open at the same time.

---

## Physical vs. Technical vs. Administrative

### Physical

Protects using physical mechanisms.

**Example:** Locked server room.

### Technical

Uses technology.

**Example:** Firewall.

### Administrative

Uses policies and procedures.

**Example:** Security policy requiring restricted server-room access.

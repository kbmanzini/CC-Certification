# Non-Repudiation

## What it means

Non-repudiation provides evidence that helps show that a specific person or system performed an action.

In simple terms:

> **Someone should not be able to credibly deny an action they actually performed.**

## Example

Imagine a company uses digital signatures when approving an important electronic document.

The signature can provide evidence that the document was approved by a particular person and that the signed information has not been improperly changed.

## Why it matters

Non-repudiation can help with:

* Accountability
* Auditing
* Investigations
* Digital transactions
* Proving the origin of information

## Technologies and Concepts

Digital signatures and appropriate logging can contribute to non-repudiation.

## Remember

**Non-repudiation = Evidence that helps prove who performed an action.**

## CC Exam Tip

If a question focuses on preventing someone from credibly denying that they performed a particular action, think:

Non-repudiation is about having trustworthy evidence connecting an action or transaction to its source, which supports accountability.
